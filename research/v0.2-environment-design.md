# V0.2 environment-generalization design (DAL-149)

**Status:** Design/pilot specification only. No V0.2 confirmatory run has occurred. Numerical checks described here are apparatus-design checks, not policy results. Keep their artifacts under `results/pilot-v0.2-design/`, never in V0.1 result directories.

## 1. Motivation and V0.1 constraints

V0.1 found no established ScientistPolicy superiority over RandomPolicy or FixedDesignPolicy. The broad fixed/random coverage performed extremely well on an identifiable two-parameter oscillator; typical successful scientist runs could be competitive, but behavioral and infrastructure failures created tail risk. V0.1 did not distinguish a useful experiment set selected in advance from benefit caused by adaptation to observations. This design does not reinterpret that result or tune environments to observed ScientistPolicy behavior.

Carry forward: (i) policies see only task description, legal controls, budget, prior actions and returned observations; truth, seeds and evaluator metadata remain evaluator-only; (ii) an accepted intervention costs one intervention, and invalid proposals consume decision opportunities under a fixed opportunity cap; (iii) world, indexed observation-noise, and policy RNG streams remain separate; (iv) preserve immutable public/provenance/evaluator artifacts and all attempts; (v) count behavioral and infrastructure failures separately and do not silently repair; (vi) score held-out trajectories fixed before policy outcomes; (vii) pilot seeds/artifacts are disjoint and visibly exploratory, and protocol, seeds, metrics and exclusions freeze before confirmation.

V0.1 assumptions requiring change: the known single equation family, oscillator-only action field names, one observed displacement, and its two-parameter fitter cannot represent coupled modes or model-class uncertainty. Its fixed four held-out probes and parameter metric are not automatically suitable. The common lifecycle, provenance, RNG ownership, failure ledger, and artifact separation should remain; generalize types only behind an unchanged policy-visible contract within each V0.2 task. Do not change V0.1 semantics.

## 2. Questions and scope

V0.2 asks whether V0.1 qualitative conclusions persist on changed systems; whether adaptation helps under different identification geometry; whether policies discriminate competing equations; and which interventions resolve ambiguity without making the task LLM-favorable. Include seeded random, predeclared fixed, adaptive ScientistPolicy, and a later predeclared open-loop LLM design control (same finite design committed before observations, executed without adaptation). No advisor/system-one condition belongs here; that is V0.3.

Use two minimal complementary tasks: (A) two-mass coupled oscillator, known structure and three unknown physical coefficients, probing modal/parameter geometry; (B) linear versus Duffing oscillator, unknown structure and common two-parameter linear baseline plus one nonlinear coefficient, probing structural identification. They are separate task conditions, not a single oversized parameter space.

## 3. Environment A: coupled damped two-mass oscillator

**Purpose/equations.** Known two-mass topology with independently controllable initial displacement and common calibrated drive on mass 1:
`m₁ ẍ₁ + c ẋ₁ + k x₁ + k_c(x₁-x₂) = u(t)`;
`m₂ ẍ₂ + c ẋ₂ + k x₂ + k_c(x₂-x₁) = 0`.
Set known masses `m₁=1 kg`, `m₂=1.5 kg` (removes scale symmetry and breaks exchange symmetry); `u(t)=A sin(2π f t)` is force in N. State `(x₁,x₂,v₁,v₂)`. Observe **both** coordinates. Since the mass matrix is asymmetric, modal eigenvectors depend on hidden `k,k_c`; fixed common/differential initializations are not generally modal, so observation-informed excitation can in principle improve the next intervention.

**Evaluator-hidden truth.** `k` (N/m), `k_c` (N/m), and `c` (N·s/m); fixed mass is public. Pilot starting supports: `k∈[1.5,4.0]`, `k_c∈[0.3,2.0]`, `c∈[0.15,0.8]`, independently uniform in physical (not log) scale, generated from the world RNG in named fixed order. These are initial bounded supports, subject to narrowing only if pilot shows degeneracy/instability. Avoid near-zero coupling: it would make coordinate 2 effectively unidentifiable for reasons unrelated to adaptive design. No hidden mode labels.

**Actions / units / legal domain.** One intervention sets `(x₁(0),x₂(0))∈[-1,1]^2 m`, `(v₁(0),v₂(0))∈[-1,1]^2 m/s`, and force amplitude `A∈[-1,1] N`, frequency `f∈[0,2] Hz`; `A=0` means unforced and requires `f=0`, otherwise `f>0`. These broad bounds are frozen as initial legal envelope, not a promise all corners are scientifically equally useful. Duration 10 s and 101 equally spaced samples per intervention initially; policy cannot vary schedule. Budget and noise exact levels are pilot-tunable; candidate budget 8 interventions and clean plus additive Gaussian coordinate noise (same marginal σ in m, independent indexed draws per time/channel/intervention).

**Observation/task contract.** Return time plus both `x₁_m`, `x₂_m` and declared noise scale; same fields for every world and action. Public task states the equations/topology, known mass, units, action ranges and schedule. It must not expose generated coefficients, modal frequencies, seeds, generation stratum, or truth-derived diagnostics. Stable validation codes only.

**Held-out evaluator targets.** Fixed evaluator-only probes (not policy-supplied): (1) x₁-only initial displacement `(1,0)`; (2) x₂-only displacement `(0,1)`; (3) opposite displacement `(1,-1)`; (4) mixed initial velocities `(0.5,-0.5)`; (5) zero state, force on mass 1 at a pilot-fixed frequency set selected from legal range independently of confirmatory worlds. Score pooled coordinate/time RMSE and normalized coefficient error; details and scaling fixed by DAL-153 after deterministic range validation. Candidate-class metric does not apply here.

**Geometry/adaptation hypothesis (not outcome claim).** A first one-coordinate displacement excites modes according to projections onto truth-dependent eigenvectors; an x₂ or opposite-sign initialization changes modal weights, and drive frequency near a weakly observed mode can add information. Because `m₁≠m₂`, neither common nor differential initialization is a known modal eigenvector across hidden truths. After seeing frequency content / relative phase in both coordinates, a second intervention may target a poorly excited mode, whereas a broad design can spend interventions on redundant initial conditions or a generic frequency grid. However, 2 coordinates observed from the beginning and 8 interventions may make fixed coverage sufficient; if modes are already well excited and coefficients well-conditioned, adaptivity has no advantage. Deterministic identifiability/conditioning checks below are required before treating this as a viable task; pilot contrasts in information geometry (not agent scores) remain necessary.

## 4. Environment B: linear versus Duffing oscillator

**Purpose/equations.** Same scalar displacement observation and identical action schema in both classes. The scientist is explicitly given the two candidate hypotheses, identically in every world:

`H_L: ẍ + 2ζω₀ ẋ + ω₀²x = a sin(2π f t)`

`H_D: ẍ + 2ζω₀ ẋ + ω₀²x + βx³ = a sin(2π f t)`

The linear class is exactly `β=0`; the Duffing class has strictly positive `β`. `x` m, time s, `a` m/s²; `β` units m⁻²s⁻². State `(x,v)`. The task asks the scientist to discriminate between these candidate classes using experiments. It does not reveal which class is true, parameter values, β, seeds, or evaluator metadata. The evaluator fits each candidate family separately.

**Hidden-world generation.** Class sampled with equal probability from a world RNG draw, independently of policy/noise; truth `ζ∈[0.08,0.30]`, `ω₀∈[0.9,1.8] rad/s` uniform for both classes; Duffing `β∈[0.15,0.8] m⁻²s⁻²` uniform. Linear `β=0` by class definition. Common parameter supports prevent class-correlated bounds from becoming a label leak. Named fixed draw order and all truth remain evaluator-only. Exact supports are pilot-tunable only if deterministic checks identify persistent confounding or numerical issues.

**Actions.** Reuse exactly the V0.1 public action fields and constraints: `x₀∈[-2,2] m`, `v₀∈[-2,2] m/s`, drive acceleration `A∈[-1,1] m/s²`, `f∈[0,3] Hz`, same zero-drive rule, 10 s/101 samples. No class-dependent limits, defaults, validation, wording, or failure paths. Candidate budget 8; clean and Gaussian additive noise σ candidates around V0.1's 0.10 m, finalized in pilot. All treatments receive identical action opportunity and observation contract.

**Observation/task contract.** `(time_s, displacement_m, uncertainty_m)` as in V0.1. Public wording gives both candidate equations, common physical units, ranges, sampling, and controls. It does not identify the hidden class, disclose parameter values or evaluator scoring, or imply that high amplitude is necessarily diagnostic. Both classes have identical artifact schemas and stable error codes.

**Held-out probes.** Fixed set includes low-amplitude unforced and driven traces, high-amplitude unforced trace, and driven high-amplitude trace at a distinct frequency; e.g. `(x₀,v₀,A,f)=(0.2,0,0,0),(0,0,0.2,0.8),(1.5,0,0,0),(0,0,0.8,1.1)`. These values are candidates, not frozen confirmatory probes: verify legal, stable and class-sensitive with deterministic checks, then freeze before confirmation. Include low-amplitude probes because they test equivalence-like ambiguity, not just winner-separation. Probe outcomes and targets remain evaluator-only.

**Geometry/adaptation.** At low displacement, cubic acceleration `βx³` is small relative to linear restoring force `ω₀²x`; data can support both classes after refitting the linear coefficients. A first low-amplitude trace estimates decay/frequency but may leave class uncertain; a rational next action raises displacement/velocity or forcing enough to expose amplitude-dependent frequency/response, then a subsequent action can test a competing frequency or repeat at controlled amplitude. Broad low-amplitude coverage wastes budget on near-equivalent trajectories; indiscriminate maximum amplitude may also be inefficient and risks saturating/noise-dominating the measurement. A predeclared mixture spanning amplitudes may already work well, and if Duffing curvature is visible even at small amplitudes then adaptivity may not help. This is not designed to guarantee an LLM win.

Linear-vs-Duffing is preferred over a larger alternative because it changes equation structure by one interpretable term, preserves a common contract, has a natural low-signal ambiguity and a controllable discriminating intervention. Its key risk is the exact nested boundary β=0 and the fact positive β can be separable under most legal amplitudes. Pilot must establish a nontrivial ambiguous region and nontrivial separable region. If no useful overlap remains without pathological bounds, reconsider pair before protocol lock rather than post-hoc selecting favorable worlds.

## 5. Structural evaluator semantics (DAL-153)

DAL-153 must predeclare a deterministic model-selection rule that accounts for the classes' differing parameter counts (for example, a Gaussian-likelihood BIC/AIC-style criterion with its likelihood/noise assumptions stated, or a specified out-of-sample criterion). It must apply the same samples, solver tolerances, deterministic initialization/refinement policy, and noise treatment to both fits. Report class-specific best-fit parameters, raw residual and convergence status; raw residual remains an auditable secondary statistic, not the primary winner rule. Exact criterion ties use a predeclared deterministic outcome (prefer `undetermined`, not arbitrary class correctness). Report correctness against evaluator class and confusion matrix separately for each true class and noise regime. Do not interpret residual or an uncalibrated criterion as posterior probability.

Report a raw evidence statistic `Δ = (S_linear - S_Duffing)/N` (or normalized equivalent frozen by DAL-153), with sign and units/normalization documented, alongside both candidate scores. A margin/abstention threshold may be used only if pilot defines it independent of confirmatory worlds; otherwise preserve continuous Δ and the winner, without calling it confidence or calibrated evidence. Held-out prediction error compares each fitted class's predictions against evaluator trajectories on fixed probes; also score the policy-selected winner's prediction. Wrong selected class is not masked: record class error and its held-out error, while retain oracle-class fit as a diagnostic. Conditional parameter error is computed against parameters for the true class only when the selected/fit class matches; for wrong class, report wrong-model parameter values descriptively, not as comparable truth error. Avoid a single composite that conceals class mistakes.

## 6. Numerical design checks and pilot boundary

The deterministic script `scripts/v0.2_design_checks.jl` explores synthetic truths and fixed legal probes; rerun via `julia +1.12.7 --project=. scripts/v0.2_design_checks.jl`. It records no policy behavior and makes no confirmatory claims. Environment A checks include repeatability, cross-excitation, and a lightweight noiseless identifiability check on 16 fixed-seed truths: bounded multistart recovery of `(k,k_c,c)` from predeclared legal starts, and finite-difference scaled sensitivity rank/condition diagnostics. They compare a single-start design with a predeclared two-start design to expose design-dependent information; these checks screen obvious degeneracy, not all practical identifiability under noise. For the illustrative Duffing truth `ζ=0.15`, `ω₀=1.3`, `β=0.5` at 10 s/101 samples, same-parameter linear RMS departure was 0.0007365 m at `x₀=0.2 m` and 0.2794 m at `x₀=1.5 m` (ratio about 379). More conservatively, after a bounded 21×21 linear nuisance-parameter grid refit, the unforced low-amplitude trace had 0.0012893 m RMSE versus 0.0855655 m for the high-amplitude trace (low fit `ζ=0.146`, `ω₀=1.305`). This is evidence of an illustrative approximate low-amplitude ambiguity and a discriminating high-amplitude intervention—not ambiguity/separation rates across proposed truth/noise distributions. This check is noiseless, uses one Duffing truth, and is not a complete comparison of fitted candidate likelihoods. Pilot must still assess nuisance confounding across support, noise, and legal designs before confirming the class pair. The public action/measurement field allowlists contain no class, truth or seed field. Save stdout/report only under a new `results/pilot-v0.2-design/` namespace if persisted. Findings are qualitative design diagnostics; all numerical cutoffs, support refinements and held-out probe selection must be revisited in the pilot.

Interpretation safeguard: optimizer residual differences on noiseless synthetic trajectories are not evidence of achievable class accuracy under measurement noise. Deterministic checks establish that equations run and identify candidate degeneracies, not that a policy can identify them.

## 7. Leakage, artifacts, and controls

At the policy boundary only task statement (including both candidate equations for Environment B), common limits/schedule, remaining budget, prior actions, safe validation status and returned noisy measurements are allowed. Do not provide world or noise seed, hidden truth, which candidate class is true, parameter values, model-specific paths/filenames, evaluator artifacts/scores, truth-derived modal frequencies, solver exceptions, or generation labels. Equal artifact keys/types and protocol versioning across class; evaluator artifact remains separately access-controlled. Audit serialized requests, safe errors, task text, file paths and logs. No true-class-coded task names/condition IDs passed to policy. World RNG draws must not depend on policy, noise draws indexed independently by intervention/channel, and policy RNG remains separate.

Preserve V0.1 style append-only raw run records and failure ledger. Record solver, tolerances, schedule, generation version, all RNG identities (evaluator side), task/action/schema/protocol versions, attempts, and candidate fit diagnostics. Pilot outputs and seeds are not reused as confirmatory worlds. Fixed, random, adaptive LLM, and open-loop LLM-design policies must share public state and action type. The open-loop design is elicited and committed before observations; its design should be scored as a policy condition without feeding results back into selection. Define its elicitation/resource accounting before DAL-156 confirmation.

## 8. Decisions frozen by DAL-149 vs pilot-tunable

**Freeze now:** the two environment families and equations; hidden-variable semantics (coupled `k,k_c,c` with known unequal masses `m₁=1,m₂=1.5 kg`; hidden linear/Duffing class and common `ζ,ω₀`, Duffing-only positive β); observations (both coordinates in A, displacement only in B); common physical controls and units; equal class prior and shared nuisance-parameter support in B; no class-specific public limits/schema/error; evaluator targets (physical coefficients/held-out trajectories in A, class correctness, two-fit residual comparison, held-out predictions and class-conditional parameter estimation in B); truth/public boundary; independent RNG ownership; failure and immutable-artifact semantics; V0.3 advisor excluded.

**Pilot-tunable (must be frozen before confirmation):** exact intervention budget (8 is candidate), noise conditions/scales, world count/repeats, final confirmatory seed set, exact narrowed physical supports only if diagnosed degeneracy, schedule only if numerical/identification checks require, fixed/open-loop designs, fixed held-out probe values, solver choice/tolerances if cross-family validation requires, class margin/abstention threshold if justified, reliability thresholds, metrics normalization, exclusions/retry rules, sample-size rationale and analysis. Pilot cannot use confirmatory outcomes and must not optimize environment against LLM performance.

## 10. Status

Design selection is provisional pending deterministic checks and the V0.2 pilot. The study can yield null, negative, or inconclusive adaptive effects. No environment or parameter range is selected to favor the ScientistPolicy, and V0.1's negative/inconclusive findings remain unchanged.
